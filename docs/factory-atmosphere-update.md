# Factory atmosphere and floor transition

The existing volumetric clouds remain the default in the local and built 2.5D scene. They use rounded density banks, sampled sun/moon shading, and a connected storm deck. This update adds strike illumination to that volume and to the actual mountain lighting, without replacing the cloud model.

The vending machine sits in the bottom-right of the front desk room, on the left side of the divider separating it from the lounge. It faces left with its display visible from the room camera. The lounge keyboard retains its original position. The machine is a procedural 3D prop with a recessed glazed display, twelve modeled drinks, individual buttons, hot/cold strips, payment reader, and retrieval tray. Its mint enamel, coral trim, lit shelves, and soft floor glow follow the factory palette. The reference was [Fuji Electric's Japanese vending machines](https://www.fujielectric.com/about/stories/detail/PJ_story_sustainable_vending_machine.html). Static parts are merged by material. Its rotated footprint is included in shared movement collision and routing; the left entrance aisle stays open.

The normal elevator transition renders both rooms together. The camera travels toward the garage view while the cutaway upper floor lifts clear; the patio rises with it. Shaft guides, slab edges, and short patio supports show the building between floors. The separate room origins are aligned during travel and the camera and garage are rebased together at arrival, preserving the final framing. Reduced motion uses the stationary fade. Walking into the lift and clicking its call control use the same visual journey.

Thunderstorm is a selectable weather preset with forked bolts, room/window light, cloud and mountain illumination, and delayed opt-in thunder. See [thunderstorm behavior](thunderstorm-weather.md).

Local review: `/prototype-25d-slice.html?skyWeather=thunderstorm&skyTime=night&controlsPreview=travel`. The existing sample controls make it possible to test the new elevator by walking without sending anything live. `floorPreview=0.5` is a development-only still of the cutaway transition.

Validation: all 662 tests across 97 files passed, production build passed, and whitespace checks passed. Browser review checked a real lightning strike, the floor animation and final room framing, retained control on a 390×844 viewport, and no horizontal overflow. Evidence: [lightning](evidence/thunderstorm-flash.png), [floor transition](evidence/floor-ascent-live.png), [mobile garage arrival](evidence/storm-garage-mobile.png). Physical-phone frame rate and subjective thunder audio quality were not measured.

Corner-placement follow-up: 33 focused tests for floor travel, routing, keyboard, cloud volume, and weather presets passed. Browser review checked clear daylight, cloudy daylight, and ordinary light rain. The subsequent front desk placement correction was checked in the storm preview; routing clears the cabinet and the lounge keyboard is back in its original position. Clouds are already weather-driven across these presets; clear has open sky, cloudy has separate sunlit banks, and rain has a thicker volume without lightning. Use `?skyWeather=cloudy&skyTime=day&skyDebug=` to compare presets through the weather & light control.
