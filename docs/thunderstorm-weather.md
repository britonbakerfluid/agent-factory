# Thunderstorm weather

Select **Thunderstorm** in the scene weather controls, or use `?skyWeather=thunderstorm`. Ordinary heavy rain stays separate. Live Open-Meteo WMO codes 95, 96 and 99 select the storm state, including when precipitation measurements are absent.

The first bolt arrives after 3–4.5 visible seconds. Subsequent strikes wait 15–29 seconds at full storm intensity. Each one uses a seeded descending leader with three tapered branches behind the mountain panorama. The patio sees the same strike. A broad light pulse enters the actual main-room and garage windows, lights the outdoor props, and drives the cloud and mountain illumination.

Thunder follows 0.85–1.95 seconds later. It is a reusable local synthesized rumble routed through the existing sound and volume controls. It sounds closer outdoors and muffled inside. No sound is started until sound is enabled. Muting or hiding the tab drops any current clap; returning to the tab starts a fresh quiet interval instead of playing missed events.

Reduced motion hides the bolt and replaces the sharp pulse with a gentle 1.8-second wash capped at 16% intensity. There is one pulse per strike, with no flashing overlay or repeated strobe. Bolt geometry buffers are reused, all added lights and geometry are disposed during hot reload, and no timers or offscreen event backlog are created.
